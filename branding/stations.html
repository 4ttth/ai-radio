ON AIR — 6 BRANDINGS READY

Six ready-to-run station identities for your AI radio app. Each is a complete imaging package — name, tagline, format, color system, logo mark, a three-DJ roster with assigned Gemini TTS voices, and sample on-air station IDs. Pick one in the app, rename it to your brand, or remix them in branding/stations.json.

Using these: in the app's Station branding panel, choose a preset — it recolors the interface and loads that station's DJ roster. Rename the station anytime; your custom name sticks. Voices (Aoede, Puck, Charon…) map to Gemini's prebuilt TTS voices, so each DJ sounds distinct. Want your own station? Copy a block in branding/stations.json and change the colors, DJs, and imaging lines.
